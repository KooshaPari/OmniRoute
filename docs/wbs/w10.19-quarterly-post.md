# W10.19 — Quarterly Contribution Post Template

This is the LinkedIn / Hashnode / personal blog post template for the end of each quarter. It compounds your reputation in public.

## Template

### Title (LinkedIn-friendly)

> My Q{quarter} {year} contributions to @diegosouzapw's OmniRoute (60K+ stars)

### Body

> Three months ago, I started contributing to **OmniRoute** — the open-source LLM gateway that powers 60K+ developers' AI workflows.
>
> Here are the {N} merged PRs I shipped this quarter:
>
> 1. **{PR title 1}** — closes #{issue}, was acknowledged for {N} days
> 2. **{PR title 2}** — fixed a {severity} bug
> 3. **{PR title 3}** — added {feature}
>
> The most interesting one was **{best PR}**, where I had to {technical challenge}. Here's what I learned:
>
> {2-3 sentence technical insight}
>
> What surprised me most was how welcoming the maintainer was to first-time contributors. If you've been thinking about contributing to an open-source project, just do it. The compounding is real.
>
> Next quarter I'm planning: {forward-looking statement}

### Hashtags

`#OpenSource` `#OmniRoute` `#LLM` `#AI` `#100DaysOfCode`

## Why post quarterly

- 4 posts/year = 4 touchpoints with your network
- Each post compounds: someone who saw Q1 will see Q2, Q3, Q4
- Recruiters and clients notice the consistency
